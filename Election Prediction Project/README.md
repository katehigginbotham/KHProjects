The objective of the project is to use different sources of political and economic information to predict the
outcome of your assigned race.
Your final prediction should include:
1. the predicted winner
2. the predicted vote shares (or vote margin)
3. an explanation of which pieces of evidence were most important for your prediction
4. a discussion of the main sources of uncertainty in your prediction.

We were assigned the NY-3 Congressional District.
